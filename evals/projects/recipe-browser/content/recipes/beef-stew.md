# Beef Stew

A hearty slow-cooked stew with tender beef and root vegetables.

## Ingredients

- 700 g beef chuck, cut into 4 cm cubes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, roughly chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 carrots, sliced
- 3 celery stalks, sliced
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 500 ml beef stock
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method

1. Season the beef generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat. Brown the beef in batches;
   set aside.
3. Reduce heat to medium. Soften the onion and garlic in the same pot, about
   5 minutes.
4. Add the carrots and celery; cook 3 minutes.
5. Stir in the tomato paste; cook 1 minute.
6. Return the beef to the pot. Add the tomatoes, stock, and thyme.
7. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook on low heat for 2–2.5 hours until the beef
   is very tender.
8. Taste and adjust seasoning before serving.

## Notes

Serve with crusty bread or mashed potatoes. Keeps well in the fridge for 4 days.
