# Miso Soup

A simple, restorative Japanese soup ready in 10 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 600 ml dashi stock (or water + 1 tsp dashi powder)
- 2 tbsp white miso paste
- 100 g silken tofu, cut into small cubes
- 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
- 1 sheet dried wakame seaweed, soaked 5 minutes and drained

## Method

1. Bring the dashi stock to a gentle simmer in a small pot — do not boil.
2. Add the tofu and wakame; warm through for 2 minutes.
3. Remove from heat. Dissolve the miso paste in a ladle with a little hot stock,
   then stir back into the pot.
4. Ladle into bowls and garnish with spring onions.

## Notes

Never boil after adding miso — it destroys the delicate flavour and kills the
beneficial bacteria. Serve immediately.
