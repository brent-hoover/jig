# Pancakes

Fluffy breakfast pancakes ready in under 20 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 200 g plain flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp sugar
- ¼ tsp salt
- 240 ml milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp melted butter, plus extra for the pan

## Method

1. Whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, beat together the milk, egg, and melted butter.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry; stir until just combined — a few
   lumps are fine.
4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and brush lightly with butter.
5. Pour roughly 60 ml of batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form on the
   surface, about 2 minutes, then flip and cook 1 minute more.
6. Serve immediately with maple syrup, berries, or jam.
